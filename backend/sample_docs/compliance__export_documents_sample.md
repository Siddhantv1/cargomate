# Export documents and compliance checklist: apparel to the UAE (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

SAMPLE DOCUMENT: a general checklist written for demonstration, not an official government list. Confirm
requirements with your customs broker.

## Registrations before the first shipment

- Importer-Exporter Code (IEC) issued by DGFT.
- Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) from the Apparel Export Promotion Council (AEPC) to claim
  DGFT benefits on apparel.
- GST registration, plus a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) to export without paying IGST, or pay IGST and claim a refund.
- An authorised-dealer bank account for export remittances.

## Documents for each shipment

- Commercial invoice stating HS code, description, quantity, unit price, FOB value and Incoterm.
- Packing list with carton counts, gross and net weight, and dimensions.
- Shipping bill filed on ICEGATE. Declare the RoDTEP claim here.
- Air waybill (air) or bill of lading (sea).
- Certificate of Origin. Under the India-UAE CEPA a preferential certificate of origin supports the lower UAE duty rate.
- Export-obligation records if capital goods were imported under EPCG.

## After shipment

- Bank realisation: e-BRC (electronic Bank Realisation Certificate) once payment is received.
- Keep copies of all documents for the retention period required by customs and GST.
