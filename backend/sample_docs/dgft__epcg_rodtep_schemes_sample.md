# DGFT schemes for apparel exporters (SAMPLE DOCUMENT)

SAMPLE DOCUMENT: a plain-language summary written for demonstration. It is not an official DGFT notification.
Replace it with the current Foreign Trade Policy, Handbook of Procedures and notified rate schedules.

## EPCG (Export Promotion Capital Goods)

EPCG lets an exporter import capital goods (for example knitting machines, embroidery machines, cutting tables) at
zero customs duty, in return for an export obligation.

- Export obligation: 6 times the duty saved, to be fulfilled within 6 years from the date the authorisation is issued.
- Eligibility: manufacturer exporters, merchant exporters tied to a supporting manufacturer, and service providers,
  each holding a valid Importer-Exporter Code (IEC).
- Applies to capital goods only. It does not give a cash incentive on exports of finished garments such as HS 6109
  T-shirts. It lowers the cost of the machinery used to make them.
- The authorisation holder must keep export-obligation records and file proof of fulfilment with DGFT.

## RoDTEP (Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products)

RoDTEP refunds embedded central, state and local duties and taxes that are not otherwise refunded, as a percentage of
the FOB value of the export, and some tariff lines carry a cap per unit.

- Rates are notified per HS code. See the RoDTEP rate schedule document for the rate and cap on each heading.
- Claimed through the shipping bill: the exporter declares the RoDTEP claim when filing it, and the credit is issued
  as a transferable duty credit scrip.
- Eligibility notes: the exporter must hold an IEC, the product must be on the notified schedule, and the export must
  not already receive a duty benefit that RoDTEP excludes.
- Rates change when DGFT issues a new notification. Always use the entry with the latest effective date.

## Estimating the benefit

RoDTEP benefit = FOB value x notified rate, limited by any per-unit cap multiplied by the number of units.
The result is informational. The credit actually issued depends on the shipping bill and the scheme conditions.
