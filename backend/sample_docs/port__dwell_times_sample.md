## Container cargo

- Mundra: vessel waiting 0.5 to 1.5 days; export container dwell 2 to 3 days.
- Nhava Sheva (JNPT): vessel waiting 1 to 2 days; export container dwell 2 to 4 days.
- Jebel Ali (Dubai): berth waiting 0.5 to 1 day; import container dwell 3 to 5 days before clearance.

## Bulk and break-bulk cargo

Bulk shipments queue for a designated berth, so waiting is longer and less predictable than for containers.

- Mundra: berth waiting 2 to 4 days for bulk shipments.
- Deendayal (Kandla): berth waiting 3 to 6 days for bulk shipments.
- Nhava Sheva (JNPT): berth waiting 2 to 3 days for break-bulk cargo.

## Seasonal notes

- During the monsoon months (June to September) west-coast ports may add 1 to 2 days of weather delay.
- Add 1 to 2 days around major holidays when customs and terminal staffing is reduced.
- Plan the cargo cut-off at least 24 hours before the vessel's published gate-closing time.
